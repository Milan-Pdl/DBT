KPI stands for Key Performance Indicator.

A KPI is a measurable value used to track how effectively a person, team, or organization is achieving a specific goal.

Breaking it down
Key: Important to the objective.
Performance: How well something is being done.
Indicator: A metric that shows progress or results.
Examples of KPIs

Sales

Monthly revenue
Number of new customers
Sales conversion rate

Marketing

Website traffic
Cost per lead
Return on advertising spend (ROAS)

Customer Service

Customer satisfaction score (CSAT)
Average response time
Customer retention rate

Human Resources

Employee turnover rate
Time to hire
Employee engagement score
Example

If a company's goal is to increase online sales by 20% this year, relevant KPIs might include:

Monthly online revenue
Website conversion rate
Average order value

These KPIs help determine whether the company is on track to achieve its goal.

In simple terms: A KPI is a number that tells you whether you're succeeding at an important objective.
